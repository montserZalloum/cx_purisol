# Feature Specification: Consumption Recording

**Feature Branch**: `004-consumption-recording`
**Created**: 2026-04-18
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: User description: "Phase 4 — Consumption Recording (Without Discrepancy Detection): record coupons handed in by delivery men at end of day. The Administrator opens a new `Purisol Coupon Consumption Entry`, picks the delivery man who is handing the coupons in, and lists the coupons either by picking them from a booklet's available-coupon checklist (Mode A) or by typing/pasting coupon numbers directly (Mode B). Both modes write to the same child table. On submit, blocking validations only — reject the whole entry if any coupon is missing, duplicated within the entry, or already consumed. On success, every listed coupon transitions `Available` → `Consumed` with `consumed_on`, `consumed_by_delivery_man`, and `consumption_entry` stamped; each affected booklet's `consumed_count` and `remaining_count` are recomputed; any booklet whose `consumed_count` reaches 20 transitions `Sold` → `Depleted` with `depleted_on` set. All side effects happen in a single transaction. Discrepancy detection (missing-coupons gap analysis, unassigned-booklet detection) and notifications are explicitly out of scope — deferred to Phases 5 and 6."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Record an End-of-Day Consumption by Picking Coupons from a Booklet (Priority: P1)

At end of day, a delivery man walks back into the shop and hands over the coupons he collected on his route. The Purisol Administrator opens a new Coupon Consumption Entry, picks the delivery man who is handing the coupons in, and — in "By Booklet" mode — types the booklet number the delivery man is working through. The system responds with a checklist of every coupon in that booklet that is still `Available`, showing each coupon's number and page position within the booklet. The Administrator ticks the coupons physically in hand and clicks "Add to Entry"; those rows appear in the main child table, each row already showing the booklet and (if set) the customer. The Administrator may repeat this for a second booklet if the delivery man brought coupons from more than one, each repetition adding more rows. Once every coupon is accounted for, the Administrator submits the entry. On submit, every listed coupon flips to `Consumed` with the entry's posting datetime, the entry's delivery man, and a back-reference to the entry; every touched booklet's aggregate counts are updated in the same transaction.

**Why this priority**: "By Booklet" mode is the primary end-of-day flow and is how the Administrator visually reconciles the physical paper stubs against the system's record. It keeps the booklet context in front of the Administrator at all times, which is how the shop actually works (coupons are handed in grouped by booklet). Until this works, no coupon can be marked `Consumed` anywhere in the system and every later Phase 4 behaviour — metadata stamping, booklet aggregates, auto-depletion — has no surface to attach to. This single slice delivers the entire phase's core value: the system has a trustworthy record of which coupons have been used, by whom, and when.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by seeding one customer, one Employee (delivery man), one booklet `WP-00001` sold to the customer via Phase 3 (so status is `Sold`, coupons `CP-00001`–`CP-00020` are `Available`). Open a new Coupon Consumption Entry, set `delivery_man` to the seeded Employee, pick `WP-00001` in Mode A, tick three coupons (for example `CP-00001`, `CP-00002`, `CP-00003`), "Add to Entry", and submit. Verify the three coupons are now `Consumed` with `consumed_on` equal to the entry's posting datetime, `consumed_by_delivery_man` equal to the entry's delivery man, and `consumption_entry` pointing back to the new entry; verify `WP-00001.consumed_count = 3` and `remaining_count = 17`; verify the remaining 17 coupons in the booklet are still `Available`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` is `Sold` to customer "Acme Co." with all 20 coupons `Available` and delivery man Ali exists as an Employee, **When** the Administrator creates a Coupon Consumption Entry with `delivery_man = Ali`, adds `CP-00001`, `CP-00002`, `CP-00003` via Mode A (pick booklet `WP-00001` → tick three boxes → "Add to Entry"), and submits, **Then** those three coupons have status `Consumed` with `consumed_on = entry.posting_datetime`, `consumed_by_delivery_man = Ali`, `consumption_entry = entry.name`; `WP-00001.consumed_count = 3`, `remaining_count = 17`; and no other coupon record is modified.
2. **Given** a delivery man brings coupons from two different booklets in the same visit, **When** the Administrator uses Mode A twice within a single Consumption Entry (once per booklet) and submits, **Then** every ticked coupon from both booklets is marked `Consumed`, each with correct `consumed_by_delivery_man` and `consumption_entry` back-reference; both booklets' aggregates are updated in the same submit.
3. **Given** the Administrator is in Mode A and enters a booklet number that does not exist or does not correspond to a real booklet record, **When** they try to use its checklist, **Then** the system surfaces a clear error identifying the booklet number as unknown; no row is added to the child table.
4. **Given** Mode A's checklist is showing `Available` coupons for a booklet, **When** the Administrator ticks zero coupons and clicks "Add to Entry", **Then** nothing is added to the child table and no error is raised — the action is simply a no-op.
5. **Given** a child-table row has been added by Mode A, **When** the Administrator clicks "remove" on that row before submitting, **Then** the row disappears from the child table and the underlying coupon's status is unchanged (no side effect happens before submit).

### User Story 2 - Record Consumption by Typing or Pasting Coupon Numbers Directly (Priority: P2)

Sometimes the Administrator has a flat list of coupon numbers in hand — for example a hand-written tally sheet or a receipt — and wants to enter them without drilling into each booklet. In "By Coupon Number" mode, the Administrator types a coupon number (or pastes several, or scans one by one with a USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner that acts as a keyboard), and on each Enter the system resolves the coupon, auto-fills the parent booklet and the customer (if the booklet is assigned), and adds a row to the same main child table Mode A uses. Mode A and Mode B may be freely mixed within a single Consumption Entry — rows from either mode carry the same shape, go through the same on-submit validations, and produce the same side effects. On submit, the behaviour is identical to Story 1: every listed coupon becomes `Consumed`, each touched booklet's aggregates update, and every side effect commits in a single transaction.

**Why this priority**: Direct coupon-number entry is the fast-path for experienced Administrators and, more importantly, is the entry point that a future barcode scanner integration plugs into (every USB/Bluetooth coupon scanner acts as a keyboard that types the scanned number then Enter). Ensuring Mode B writes into the same child table as Mode A means the rest of the system — submit-time validation, metadata stamping, aggregate recomputation, auto-depletion — does not need to know which mode was used. This keeps the data model simple and makes the future barcode-scanner rollout (explicitly called out as future-proofed in PRD 12.5 and 15.4) a no-op for everything downstream of the input box.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by seeding one customer, one delivery man (Employee), and two booklets both `Sold` with several `Available` coupons. Open a Coupon Consumption Entry, set the delivery man, switch to Mode B, and type the numbers of four coupons drawn from both booklets (e.g. two `CP-nnnnn` from `WP-00001` and two from `WP-00002`), pressing Enter after each. Verify four rows land in the main child table with correct auto-filled booklet and customer. Submit. Verify all four coupons transition to `Consumed` with identical metadata to Mode A, and that both booklets' `consumed_count` increments by 2 each.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** booklets `WP-00001` (customer "Acme Co.") and `WP-00002` (customer "Beta Ltd.") are `Sold` with all coupons `Available`, **When** the Administrator creates a Consumption Entry, sets `delivery_man = Ali`, switches to Mode B, and types `CP-00001`, `CP-00022` one per line pressing Enter between each, **Then** two rows appear in the main child table: the first with `coupon = CP-00001`, `booklet = WP-00001`, `customer = Acme Co.`; the second with `coupon = CP-00022`, `booklet = WP-00002`, `customer = Beta Ltd.`; on submit both coupons are `Consumed`, each booklet's `consumed_count` increments by 1.
2. **Given** the Administrator is in Mode B and types a coupon number that does not exist in the system (e.g. `CP-99999`), **When** they press Enter, **Then** the system surfaces a clear error naming the unknown coupon; no row is added to the child table, and existing rows are untouched.
3. **Given** the Administrator has already added three rows via Mode A, **When** they switch to Mode B and add two more coupons by number, **Then** the main child table holds all five rows with consistent shape (coupon, booklet, customer) regardless of entry mode; submit processes all five identically.
4. **Given** the Administrator pastes a multi-line block of coupon numbers into the Mode B input, **When** the block is processed, **Then** each valid line adds one row to the child table and each invalid line surfaces a per-line error; valid lines remain in the table so the Administrator only has to correct the flagged lines.
5. **Given** the Administrator adds coupon `CP-00005` via Mode B and later re-enters `CP-00005` in the same entry (either by Mode A, Mode B, or paste), **When** they submit, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear "duplicate coupon in entry" error identifying `CP-00005` (per Story 4); no coupon is modified.

### User Story 3 - Auto-Deplete a Booklet When Its 20th Coupon Is Consumed (Priority: P2)

Booklets always have exactly 20 coupons. The moment the 20th and final coupon is marked `Consumed`, the booklet has served its purpose and its lifecycle ends: the system automatically transitions the booklet from `Sold` to `Depleted` and stamps `depleted_on` with the moment the transition happened. This is not a separate admin action — it is a guaranteed side effect of the Consumption Entry submit that contains the 20th coupon. Whether the booklet reaches 20 via a single entry (all 20 coupons submitted together) or across many entries spanning many days, the transition fires exactly once, in the same transaction as the coupon updates, so no intermediate state is ever visible where `consumed_count = 20` but `status != Depleted`.

**Why this priority**: Auto-depletion is the system's signal that a customer has finished a booklet and is a candidate for resale follow-up. It is also the precondition that unlocks Phase 6's "Booklet Depleted" notification and Phase 7's lifecycle reports — neither can fire or compute without this transition actually happening. Making it automatic (rather than an admin action) removes an easy-to-forget manual step and guarantees that the `Depleted` status is never out of sync with the underlying coupon counts. It is P2 rather than P1 because it sits on top of Story 1's consumption-recording mechanics; until Story 1 works, there are no consumed coupons for this story to react to.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by seeding one booklet `WP-00001` `Sold` with 19 of 20 coupons already `Consumed` and one coupon (`CP-00020`) still `Available`. Create a Consumption Entry that records `CP-00020` (via either mode) and submit. Verify `CP-00020` is now `Consumed`, `WP-00001.consumed_count = 20`, `remaining_count = 0`, `status = Depleted`, and `depleted_on` is populated with a timestamp at or after the entry's posting datetime. Separately verify the single-entry case: seed a booklet with all 20 coupons `Available`, submit one entry that lists all 20, and verify the booklet ends up `Depleted` with exactly one transition in its change log.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` is `Sold` with 19 coupons already `Consumed` and `CP-00020` still `Available`, **When** the Administrator submits a Consumption Entry containing only `CP-00020`, **Then** within the same submit transaction `CP-00020` becomes `Consumed`, `WP-00001.consumed_count = 20`, `remaining_count = 0`, `status = Depleted`, and `depleted_on` is stamped; no intermediate state where `consumed_count = 20` but `status = Sold` is ever observable.
2. **Given** booklet `WP-00002` is `Sold` with all 20 coupons `Available`, **When** the Administrator submits a single Consumption Entry that lists all 20 coupons of that booklet, **Then** on submit all 20 coupons become `Consumed` and the booklet transitions `Sold` → `Depleted` exactly once, with `depleted_on` populated.
3. **Given** a Consumption Entry lists coupons drawn from several booklets and the submit drives one of those booklets to `consumed_count = 20` while another is only partially consumed, **When** the entry is submitted, **Then** only the fully-consumed booklet transitions to `Depleted`; the partially-consumed booklet remains `Sold` with its new counts reflected.
4. **Given** a booklet is already `Depleted` (all 20 coupons `Consumed`), **When** the Administrator tries to add any coupon from that booklet to a new Consumption Entry, **Then** the coupon does not appear in Mode A's "available coupons" checklist and is rejected with an "already consumed" error if entered by number in Mode B or submitted through any other path.
5. **Given** auto-depletion has fired on a booklet, **When** the Administrator opens that booklet's record, **Then** the change log attributes the status change to the specific Consumption Entry that contained the 20th coupon, so any later viewer can trace from the `Depleted` booklet back to the triggering entry without external context.

### User Story 4 - Reject Invalid Submissions With Clear Errors, All-or-Nothing (Priority: P3)

Data integrity is the whole point of this system. Before any coupon is touched, the Consumption Entry submit must check every row against three blocking conditions: the referenced coupon must actually exist, no coupon may appear twice in the same entry, and no coupon may already be in status `Consumed`. If any row fails any check, the entire submit is rejected — not partially — with an error message that names the offending coupon(s) and the specific reason. No coupon's status changes, no booklet's counts change, no depletion fires; the Administrator sees the failed draft with its rows intact, corrects the bad rows, and tries again. This all-or-nothing guarantee is what makes the consumption log trustworthy: if a submit succeeded, every listed coupon is truly `Consumed`; if it failed, nothing happened at all. Non-blocking warnings (missing-coupons gap detection, unassigned-booklet detection) are explicitly deferred to Phase 5 and must not be implemented here.

**Why this priority**: Validation is essential for trust in the dataset, but functionally it sits below the happy-path stories — Stories 1, 2, and 3 must already work before there is anything meaningful to validate against. Grouping all three blocking checks and the transactional-atomicity guarantee into one story keeps them testable as a coherent safety net rather than as scattered edge cases. It is P3 because the happy path (Stories 1–3) is what delivers user value; Story 4 protects that value but does not create new capability.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested after Stories 1–3. For each rejection path, build a draft Consumption Entry that triggers exactly that path, submit it, verify the error message is clear and names the problem row(s), and verify the pre-submit state of every coupon and booklet is exactly preserved (nothing moved to `Consumed`, no counts changed, no `Depleted` transition). Specifically: (a) list a `CP-nnnnn` that does not exist → rejected, no side effect; (b) list the same coupon twice in one entry → rejected, no side effect; (c) list a coupon whose status is already `Consumed` → rejected, no side effect; (d) an entry with 10 valid rows and one bad row → all 10 valid rows are untouched after rejection.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the Administrator builds a Consumption Entry containing a mix of valid coupon references and one row whose coupon number points to no existing record, **When** they submit, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear error naming the missing coupon number; no coupon in the entry is marked `Consumed` and no booklet's counts change.
2. **Given** a Consumption Entry contains the same coupon number in two different rows (one added by Mode A, one by Mode B, or any other combination), **When** the Administrator submits, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear "duplicate coupon in entry" error identifying the repeated coupon; no coupon in the entry is modified.
3. **Given** a Consumption Entry lists a coupon whose current status is `Consumed` (because it was recorded on an earlier entry), **When** the Administrator submits, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear "coupon already consumed" error naming the coupon and ideally referencing the earlier Consumption Entry that consumed it; no other coupon in the entry is modified.
4. **Given** a Consumption Entry with 10 valid rows and 1 invalid row fails submit for any of the blocking reasons, **When** the Administrator removes or fixes the offending row and submits again, **Then** the resubmitted entry succeeds and all 10 (or now 11) rows transition to `Consumed` together; the earlier failure has left no trace on any coupon or booklet.
5. **Given** a Consumption Entry includes a coupon whose parent booklet is not currently `Sold` (it is still `In Stock`, `In Custody`, or `Depleted`), **When** the Administrator submits, **Then** in Phase 4 the submit does NOT block on this condition (discrepancy detection is deferred to Phase 5); the coupon is marked `Consumed` normally. Phase 5 will later add a non-blocking warning for this case.
6. **Given** a Consumption Entry with zero rows in its child table, **When** the Administrator tries to submit, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear "consumption entry must contain at least one coupon" error; an empty entry has no meaning.

### Edge Cases

- **Consumption Entry created but never submitted**: A draft entry holds rows but has no side effects. If the Administrator abandons the draft, no coupon or booklet is affected; the draft can be discarded freely.
- **Two drafts both listing the same coupon**: Draft state is not a reservation. If two drafts both list `CP-00005`, whichever submits first succeeds and marks it `Consumed`; the second submit fails on the "already consumed" blocking check (Story 4).
- **Coupon from a booklet currently `In Custody` or `In Stock`**: Per PRD this should become an `Unassigned Booklet` discrepancy warning, but in Phase 4 that detection is explicitly deferred to Phase 5. The Consumption Entry submits normally; the coupon becomes `Consumed` without any warning record. Phase 5 layers the warning on top of the existing Phase 4 submit logic without blocking it.
- **Coupon number that creates a gap in the booklet's consumption sequence** (e.g. submitting `CP-00007`, `CP-00008` when `CP-00003`–`CP-00006` are still `Available`): No discrepancy is detected in Phase 4. Phase 5 adds gap analysis on top.
- **Delivery man field left blank**: Required field — the entry cannot be submitted without a `delivery_man`. Error surfaces at the form level before any validation runs, matching standard Frappe required-field behaviour.
- **Posting datetime in the future or far in the past**: The entry accepts whatever posting datetime the Administrator sets (Frappe default is `now()`); the stamped `consumed_on` on each coupon matches that value. Admin judgement handles backdating / future-dating.
- **Submitting a very large entry (hundreds of coupons at once)**: The transactional-atomicity requirement still holds — either all rows succeed or none do. Typical operational volume is under 200 coupons/day so this is not expected, but the behaviour must not silently degrade.
- **Cancellation of a submitted Consumption Entry**: Standard Frappe submittable semantics apply — cancelling reverts every listed coupon from `Consumed` back to `Available`, clears `consumed_on`, `consumed_by_delivery_man`, `consumption_entry` on those coupons, recomputes each affected booklet's `consumed_count` and `remaining_count`, and — if this entry had triggered a booklet's transition to `Depleted` — reverts that booklet back to `Sold` with `depleted_on` cleared. Cancellation is all-or-nothing: if any reversal would itself be invalid, the cancellation is rejected with a clear error and no coupon is modified.
- **Amending a submitted Consumption Entry**: Handled via standard Frappe cancel + amend. The cancel step follows the rules above; the amendment is a fresh entry that goes through the same submit validations.
- **Mode B entry where the pasted block contains blank lines or whitespace**: Blank lines are ignored silently; lines with only whitespace are treated as blank. Non-blank lines that cannot be parsed as a coupon number surface a clear per-line error.
- **Non-Administrator attempts**: Users without the `Purisol Administrator` role must be unable to create, write, or submit a Consumption Entry. Standard Frappe role permissions enforce this.
- **A coupon is deleted from the system between draft and submit**: An already-added draft row referencing a now-deleted coupon is rejected at submit with the "coupon does not exist" blocking error (Story 4, scenario 1); the Administrator rebuilds the row.
- **Booklet is `Depleted` before the draft submits** (by an intervening entry): Any draft row referencing one of that booklet's coupons is rejected at submit with the "already consumed" blocking error; the Administrator rebuilds the draft.
- **Concurrent submit of two entries that both list the same coupon**: Exactly one submit succeeds; the other is rejected with the "already consumed" blocking error at submit-time validation.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### Consumption Entry DocType & Form

- **FR-001**: The system MUST provide a new submittable DocType `Purisol Coupon Consumption Entry` with a `PCC-.YYYY.-.#####` naming series, carrying at minimum the following fields: `posting_date` (Date, required, default today), `posting_time` (Time, required, default now), `delivery_man` (Link → Employee, required — the delivery man who is handing the coupons in today), `received_by` (Link → User, read-only — the Administrator recording the entry), `coupons` (child table, required), `total_coupons` (Int, read-only — computed as the row count of `coupons`), `has_warnings` (Check, read-only — reserved for Phase 5; always `false` in Phase 4), `discrepancies_detected` (child table, read-only — reserved for Phase 5; always empty in Phase 4), and `notes` (Small Text, optional). Track Changes MUST be enabled.
- **FR-002**: The system MUST provide a new child-table DocType `Purisol Coupon Consumption Item` with the fields: `coupon` (Link → Purisol Coupon, required), `booklet` (Link → Purisol Coupon Booklet, read-only, auto-fetched from `coupon.booklet`), `customer` (Link → Customer, read-only, auto-fetched from `coupon.booklet.customer` and MAY be empty if the parent booklet is not `Sold`).
- **FR-003**: The Consumption Entry form MUST expose a tab-style mode selector offering "By Booklet" (Mode A) and "By Coupon Number" (Mode B). Both modes MUST write their output into the same `coupons` child table so that downstream logic is mode-agnostic.

#### Mode A — "By Booklet" Input

- **FR-010**: In Mode A, the system MUST provide a booklet-number input with autocomplete over existing `Purisol Coupon Booklet` records. Selecting a booklet MUST render a checklist of every coupon in that booklet whose current `status` is `Available`, displayed with both the coupon number (e.g. `CP-00007`) and its `page_number` (1–20) within the booklet.
- **FR-011**: In Mode A, the system MUST provide an "Add to Entry" action that appends one child-table row per ticked coupon, with `coupon`, `booklet`, and `customer` populated. An "Add to Entry" click with zero ticks MUST be a no-op (no error, no row added).
- **FR-012**: In Mode A, entering a booklet number that does not correspond to an existing `Purisol Coupon Booklet` MUST surface a clear error identifying the unknown booklet number; no checklist is rendered and no child-table row is added.
- **FR-013**: Mode A MAY be used repeatedly within a single Consumption Entry to add coupons from different booklets; each use appends additional rows to the same child table.

#### Mode B — "By Coupon Number" Input

- **FR-020**: In Mode B, the system MUST provide a coupon-number input that accepts a single coupon per Enter keypress AND accepts a paste of multiple coupon numbers (one per line, blank lines ignored). On each valid entry the system MUST append one row to the `coupons` child table with `coupon`, `booklet`, and `customer` auto-populated from the referenced coupon record.
- **FR-021**: In Mode B, an entry that does not resolve to an existing `Purisol Coupon` MUST NOT add a row and MUST surface a clear per-line error identifying the unknown coupon number.
- **FR-022**: Mode B input fields MUST accept keyboard input and Enter-to-commit in a way that is transparent to USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners acting as keyboards (forward-compatibility for the future barcode-scanning rollout — no additional logic required in this phase beyond standard keyboard-input acceptance).

#### Submit-Time Blocking Validations

- **FR-030**: On submit, the system MUST reject the entry — with no state changes to any coupon or booklet — if the `coupons` child table is empty.
- **FR-031**: On submit, the system MUST reject the entry if any row's `coupon` does not resolve to an existing `Purisol Coupon` record. The error MUST name the offending coupon reference.
- **FR-032**: On submit, the system MUST reject the entry if the same `coupon` value appears in two or more rows of the same entry. The error MUST name the duplicated coupon.
- **FR-033**: On submit, the system MUST reject the entry if any referenced coupon has current status `Consumed`. The error MUST name the offending coupon. The error message SHOULD also reference the prior `consumption_entry` that consumed it (diagnostic aid).
- **FR-034**: Submit MUST be all-or-nothing: if any blocking validation fails on any row, the submit fails atomically. No coupon is marked `Consumed`, no booklet's `consumed_count` or `remaining_count` changes, and no booklet transitions to `Depleted`.
- **FR-035**: Phase 4 MUST NOT implement any non-blocking warning: no "missing-coupons" gap detection, no "unassigned booklet" detection, no creation of `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` records, no `has_warnings = true`, no populated `discrepancies_detected`. These behaviours are reserved for Phase 5 and must integrate on top of this spec without breaking the Phase 4 submit path.

#### Submit-Time Success Side Effects

- **FR-040**: When every blocking validation passes, the system MUST — in a single database transaction — update every listed coupon: `status` = `Consumed`, `consumed_on` = entry's `posting_datetime` (the combination of `posting_date` and `posting_time`), `consumed_by_delivery_man` = entry's `delivery_man`, `consumption_entry` = entry's `name`.
- **FR-041**: In the same transaction, for each booklet touched by the entry (distinct booklets across all rows), the system MUST recompute `consumed_count` (count of the booklet's coupons with `status = Consumed`) and `remaining_count` (`total_coupons - consumed_count`, which equals `20 - consumed_count`).
- **FR-042**: In the same transaction, for each booklet whose recomputed `consumed_count` reaches 20, the system MUST transition the booklet `Sold` → `Depleted` and stamp `depleted_on` with the moment the transition happened. A booklet that reaches 20 via this entry MUST NOT be observable in an intermediate state where `consumed_count = 20` but `status != Depleted`.
- **FR-043**: The system MUST attribute the side effects on each affected coupon and booklet to this Consumption Entry in the audit trail (Track Changes), so that any later viewer can answer "why did this coupon become Consumed?" or "why did this booklet become Depleted?" from the target record alone, without external context.
- **FR-044**: Phase 4 MUST NOT fire any notification (customer low-stock, booklet depleted, warehouse low-stock, new discrepancy). The notification layer is Phase 6 and MUST be able to plug in on top of the Phase 4 submit path without modifying it.

#### Cancel-Time Behaviour

- **FR-050**: On cancellation of a submitted Consumption Entry, the system MUST — in a single transaction — revert every listed coupon: `status` from `Consumed` back to `Available`; clear `consumed_on`, `consumed_by_delivery_man`, `consumption_entry`.
- **FR-051**: In the same transaction, the system MUST recompute `consumed_count` and `remaining_count` for every booklet touched by the cancelled entry.
- **FR-052**: In the same transaction, if this Consumption Entry had driven any booklet to `Depleted`, the system MUST revert that booklet's `status` from `Depleted` back to `Sold` and clear `depleted_on`, provided no other consumption entry has since pushed `consumed_count` back to 20. If another entry now holds that booklet at 20, the booklet remains `Depleted`.
- **FR-053**: Cancellation MUST be all-or-nothing. If any reversal cannot proceed (data-integrity failure), the cancellation is rejected with a clear error and no coupon or booklet is modified.

#### Data Integrity & Transactional Atomicity

- **FR-060**: Every multi-record update driven by submit (FR-040, FR-041, FR-042) and by cancel (FR-050, FR-051, FR-052) MUST commit within a single database transaction. Partial completion MUST NOT be visible to any reader at any time.
- **FR-061**: The system MUST tolerate concurrent submits of two entries that reference the same coupon: exactly one succeeds; the other is rejected at FR-033's "already consumed" blocking check. The rejection MUST NOT leave the coupon in an inconsistent state.

#### Permissions

- **FR-070**: Only users with the `Purisol Administrator` role MUST be able to create, write, submit, cancel, or amend a `Purisol Coupon Consumption Entry`.
- **FR-071**: The `received_by` field MUST be populated automatically with the user creating the entry and MUST NOT be editable by any user.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Purisol Coupon Consumption Entry (new)**: The daily record of coupons a given delivery man handed in. Submittable. Carries the delivery-man attribution, the posting datetime, the child table of consumed coupons, plus the Phase-5-reserved fields (`has_warnings`, `discrepancies_detected`). Track Changes on. One-to-many with `Purisol Coupon` (via the child table).
- **Purisol Coupon Consumption Item (new child table)**: One row per coupon being recorded in the entry. Holds a Link to the coupon plus two fetched-on-select fields: the parent booklet (from `coupon.booklet`) and the customer (from `coupon.booklet.customer`, possibly empty). Created by either Mode A or Mode B; the stored row is mode-agnostic.
- **Purisol Coupon (existing, from Phase 1)**: On Consumption Entry submit, `status` transitions `Available` → `Consumed`, and the coupon's `consumed_on`, `consumed_by_delivery_man`, `consumption_entry` fields are populated. On Consumption Entry cancel, the inverse is performed. Phase 4 does not change the coupon schema.
- **Purisol Coupon Booklet (existing, from Phase 1)**: On submit, `consumed_count` and `remaining_count` are recomputed for each booklet touched by the entry; a booklet whose `consumed_count` reaches 20 transitions `Sold` → `Depleted` with `depleted_on` stamped. On cancel, the inverse is performed where safe. Phase 4 does not change the booklet schema.
- **Purisol Coupon Discrepancy (reserved)**: Referenced in the Consumption Entry's `discrepancies_detected` child table but NOT populated in Phase 4. The field and child-table structure exist only to keep Phase 5's integration additive.
- **Employee (ERPNext standard, leveraged)**: The `delivery_man` on the entry and each coupon's `consumed_by_delivery_man` both link to Employee. Read only; not modified by this feature.
- **Customer (ERPNext standard, leveraged)**: Auto-fetched into each child-table row from the parent booklet for display and reference. Read only; not modified by this feature.
- **User (ERPNext standard, leveraged)**: Auto-stamped on the entry as `received_by`. Read only; not modified by this feature.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: An Administrator can record a typical end-of-day consumption for one delivery man covering 10–20 coupons across 1–3 booklets (using either mode or a mix) in under 90 seconds, ending with a submitted Consumption Entry, every listed coupon `Consumed` with correct metadata, and every touched booklet's aggregates updated.
- **SC-002**: After any successful Consumption Entry submit, 100% of listed coupons have `status = Consumed`, `consumed_on` equal to the entry's posting datetime, `consumed_by_delivery_man` equal to the entry's delivery man, and `consumption_entry` pointing back to the new entry — verifiable by inspecting any affected coupon record immediately.
- **SC-003**: After any successful Consumption Entry submit, 100% of touched booklets have `consumed_count` exactly equal to the current count of their `Consumed` coupons and `remaining_count = 20 - consumed_count` — no drift between aggregate counts and underlying coupon statuses, ever.
- **SC-004**: 100% of booklets whose post-submit `consumed_count` equals 20 transition to `Depleted` with `depleted_on` populated within the same submit transaction; no booklet with `consumed_count = 20` and `status != Depleted` is ever observable in the database.
- **SC-005**: 100% of submits with any blocking violation (duplicate coupon within entry, already-consumed coupon, non-existent coupon, empty child table) are rejected before any coupon or booklet is modified. Verified by attempting each rejection path and asserting that every coupon and booklet in the attempted entry's scope is bit-for-bit identical to its pre-submit state.
- **SC-006**: Validation error messages for every rejection path (FR-030 through FR-033) allow a first-time Administrator to identify and correct the problem without consulting external documentation — verified by having a non-author tester resolve each rejection path on first read.
- **SC-007**: Both input modes produce structurally identical child-table rows: for any given set of coupons, the rows added by Mode A and the rows added by Mode B are indistinguishable by downstream logic. Verified by constructing the same entry twice (once via Mode A, once via Mode B) and asserting identical submit behaviour and identical post-submit state.
- **SC-008**: Cancelling a submitted Consumption Entry restores 100% of its listed coupons to `status = Available` with consumption metadata cleared, restores 100% of its touched booklets' `consumed_count` and `remaining_count` to their pre-submit values, and reverts any entry-triggered `Depleted` booklet back to `Sold` with `depleted_on` cleared — in a single transaction, all-or-nothing. Verified by round-tripping (seed → submit → cancel) and asserting every field matches the pre-submit baseline.
- **SC-009**: Concurrent submits of two entries that reference the same coupon result in exactly one success and one rejection (via the "already consumed" blocking check), with no partial state left by the rejected submit. Verified by a concurrency test that launches two simultaneous submits and asserts the final-state invariants.
- **SC-010**: Every coupon that becomes `Consumed` and every booklet that becomes `Depleted` carries an audit-trail entry attributing the change to the specific Consumption Entry responsible, so any later viewer can answer "why did this status change?" from the target record alone.
- **SC-011**: Phase 4 introduces zero discrepancy records and zero notifications. Verified by asserting that after any Phase-4 submit, the count of `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` records is exactly what it was before the submit and that no `Notification Log` entry has been created by this flow. Phase 5 and Phase 6 will later layer those behaviours on top without altering Phase 4's invariants.

## Assumptions

- **Phases 1, 2, and 3 are in place**: `Purisol Coupon Booklet`, `Purisol Coupon`, `Purisol Settings`, `Purisol Custody Entry`, and the Sales Invoice integration from Phase 3 are fully implemented. A booklet can be in states `In Stock`, `In Custody`, `Sold`, or `Depleted`; a coupon can be `Available` or `Consumed`; the `consumed_on`, `consumed_by_delivery_man`, `consumption_entry` fields exist on `Purisol Coupon` from Phase 1; the `consumed_count`, `remaining_count`, `depleted_on` fields exist on `Purisol Coupon Booklet` from Phase 1. No schema changes to those existing DocTypes are required by Phase 4.
- **Delivery-man attribution is flat**: The `consumed_by_delivery_man` stamped on each coupon is the entry's `delivery_man` — i.e., the person who physically handed the coupon in today. This is independent of the booklet's (now-empty, since custody ends at sale) `current_delivery_man`. The constitution and PRD agree on this.
- **Custody ends at sale (from Phase 3)**: A booklet does not have a `current_delivery_man` after it is `Sold`. Consumed coupons are therefore attributed solely to the `delivery_man` on the Consumption Entry, regardless of any prior custody chain.
- **Discrepancy detection is Phase 5**: "Missing Coupons" (gap in consumption sequence) and "Unassigned Booklet" (coupon from a booklet whose status is not `Sold`) are both explicit Phase 5 scope. Phase 4 must not implement them, even partially. Phase 5 is designed to extend the Phase 4 submit path additively — it must not require a rewrite of Phase 4.
- **Notifications are Phase 6**: "Customer Low Stock", "Booklet Depleted", "Warehouse Low Stock", and "New Discrepancy Opened" are all Phase 6 scope. Phase 4 must not fire any of them. The notification abstraction layer plugs in on top of the Phase 4 submit path without altering it.
- **Reports are Phase 7**: Phase 4 does not build the "Coupon Consumption Log" or "Daily Delivery Man Summary" reports. Those consume the data Phase 4 produces but are delivered later.
- **Dual-mode form widget acceptance**: The dual-mode input form (Mode A + Mode B) is achievable within standard Frappe form / client-script capabilities. No custom single-page-app is assumed.
- **Typical operational volume**: A single Consumption Entry is expected to hold up to ~200 coupons on a busy day (one delivery man's full route). The transactional-atomicity requirements (FR-060) hold at that scale without a background job. Bulk-day operations of thousands of coupons across many delivery men are not a target.
- **Barcode scanning is future work**: Phase 4 does not integrate with barcode scanners, but Mode B's input fields must accept paste and keyboard-plus-Enter input so that a USB/Bluetooth scanner acting as a keyboard works out of the box when introduced later. PRD 12.5 and 15.4 already commit to this design.
- **Arabic + English rendering**: All user-facing strings (labels, placeholders, error messages, confirmation dialogs) are localizable via `frappe._()` per the constitution. Both LTR (English) and RTL (Arabic) must render correctly.
- **Single-shop, single-currency**: One shop, one base currency. Multi-branch and multi-currency are explicit non-goals for the MVP and do not affect Phase 4 logic.
- **Test data**: The `setup_test_data` helper from Phase 1 (extended in Phases 2 and 3) is reused to seed customers, employees (delivery men), booklets, and coupons in known states for Phase 4's tests; Phase 4 tests extend the seed rather than redefining it.
