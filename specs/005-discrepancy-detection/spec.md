# Feature Specification: Discrepancy Detection & Resolution

**Feature Branch**: `005-discrepancy-detection`
**Created**: 2026-04-18
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: User description: "Phase 5 — Discrepancy Detection & Resolution: add the system's 'intelligence' on top of Phase 4's consumption recording. During a Coupon Consumption Entry submit, after blocking validations pass and after the core consume-coupon side effects commit, the system scans each affected booklet for two anomalies: (a) **Missing Coupons** — any gap between the lowest and highest consumed coupon numbers in that booklet where the in-between coupons are still `Available`; and (b) **Unassigned Booklet** — any coupon submitted from a booklet whose status is not `Sold`. For each anomaly detected, the system creates a `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` submittable record (status `Open`) linking back to the triggering consumption entry, the booklet, the customer (if any), and the affected coupons, with an auto-computed `estimated_amount` and an auto-populated `related_delivery_men` table listing the booklet's current custody holder and every delivery man who submitted consumption entries for the same booklet in the last 30 days. The consumption entry itself is flagged with `has_warnings = True`, its `discrepancies_detected` child table is populated, and a non-blocking modal dialog tells the Administrator what was opened. A separate resolution workflow lets the Administrator open any `Open` discrepancy and submit it with one of three `resolution_action` values: `None` (Admin Error, no financial consequence), `Add to Liability Ledger` (creates a Journal Entry debiting the liable delivery man's employee-liability account and crediting the discrepancy-offset account), or `Immediate Cash Payment` (creates a Payment Entry receiving cash against the discrepancy-offset account). Resolution requires `liable_delivery_man` for the two financial paths. Once a discrepancy is submitted, it is locked. Notifications (Phase 6) and reports (Phase 7) are explicitly out of scope."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Auto-Detect Missing Coupons in a Booklet's Sequence (Priority: P1)

During end-of-day consumption recording, the Administrator submits coupons `CP-00007` and `CP-00008` from booklet `WP-00001`. Coupons `CP-00003`, `CP-00004`, `CP-00005`, and `CP-00006` from the same booklet are still `Available` (never consumed). That in-between gap — whose endpoints are themselves consumed but whose interior is not — is the signature of lost or withheld coupons, and it is exactly what Phase 5 looks for. The consumption entry still succeeds: every coupon the Administrator listed flips to `Consumed`, booklet aggregates update, and auto-depletion runs as usual — all the Phase 4 behaviour is preserved. But in the same transaction, the system creates a new `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` record with `discrepancy_type = "Missing Coupons"`, status `Open`, linked to the consumption entry, the booklet, and the booklet's customer, with the four missing coupons (`CP-00003`–`CP-00006`) listed in `affected_coupons` and an `estimated_amount` auto-computed from the booklet's per-coupon price. The Administrator sees a non-blocking modal dialog on submit listing every discrepancy that was just opened, and the discrepancy now sits in the "Open Discrepancies" list view awaiting investigation.

**Why this priority**: Missing-coupons detection is the single highest-value behaviour of the entire Phase 5 feature — it is the system's primary fraud / loss signal. Without it, lost or skimmed coupons simply never surface: Phase 4 faithfully records what was handed in, but has no notion of "what should have been handed in first". P1 because this is the one capability whose absence would make the whole phase not worth shipping, and because it is the anomaly the shop owner most needs automated (humans do not spot gaps reliably). The detection also has to live inside the consumption entry's submit transaction, so the record of "what was consumed" and the record of "what went missing in the process" cannot drift apart.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by seeding one customer, one booklet `WP-00001` sold to that customer (all 20 coupons `Available`), and one delivery man. Submit a first Coupon Consumption Entry for `CP-00001`, `CP-00002` — verify no discrepancy is created (no gap: the consumed set is `{1,2}`, no gap between min and max). Submit a second Consumption Entry for `CP-00007`, `CP-00008` — verify exactly one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` record is created with `discrepancy_type = "Missing Coupons"`, `status = "Open"`, `booklet = WP-00001`, `customer` equal to the booklet's customer, `triggering_consumption_entry` pointing at the second entry, and `affected_coupons` containing exactly `CP-00003`, `CP-00004`, `CP-00005`, `CP-00006`; verify `estimated_amount = 4 × coupon_unit_price`; verify the second consumption entry has `has_warnings = True` and its `discrepancies_detected` table references the new discrepancy.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` is `Sold` with all 20 coupons `Available`, **When** the Administrator submits a Consumption Entry listing only `CP-00007` and `CP-00008`, **Then** exactly one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` of type `Missing Coupons` is created with `affected_coupons = [CP-00003, CP-00004, CP-00005, CP-00006]`, `status = Open`, `booklet = WP-00001`, `customer` equal to the booklet's customer, and `triggering_consumption_entry` equal to the submitted entry; the entry itself still succeeds (the two listed coupons are `Consumed`), `has_warnings` is `True`, and the new discrepancy appears in the entry's `discrepancies_detected` child table.
2. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` already has coupons `CP-00001`, `CP-00002` consumed from a previous entry (no gap), **When** the Administrator submits a new Consumption Entry for `CP-00007`, `CP-00008`, **Then** the detection logic considers the union of previously-consumed and newly-consumed coupons (`{1,2,7,8}`), identifies the gap as `{3,4,5,6}` (coupons still `Available`), and creates a single Missing Coupons discrepancy listing those four coupons.
3. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` has `CP-00001`–`CP-00010` all consumed contiguously, **When** the Administrator submits a Consumption Entry for `CP-00011`, `CP-00012`, **Then** no Missing Coupons discrepancy is created — the union is `{1..12}`, min=1, max=12, no interior gaps.
4. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` already has an `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy listing `CP-00003`, `CP-00004`, `CP-00005`, `CP-00006`, **When** the Administrator submits a later Consumption Entry for `CP-00009`, `CP-00010` (which would re-detect the same `{3,4,5,6}` gap plus a possible new one), **Then** the already-listed coupons are excluded from any new discrepancy — no duplicate Missing Coupons record is created for coupons already covered by an Open discrepancy for the same booklet.
5. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` has `CP-00001`–`CP-00008` already consumed and an `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy covering `CP-00009`, `CP-00010` (previously skipped), **When** the Administrator submits a new Consumption Entry for `CP-00012`, **Then** a new Missing Coupons discrepancy is created listing only `CP-00011` (the newly-revealed gap element), because `CP-00009`, `CP-00010` are already on an Open discrepancy.
6. **Given** a Consumption Entry touches two different booklets each with its own gap (e.g. `WP-00001` gap `{3,4,5,6}` and `WP-00002` gap `{2,3}`), **When** the entry is submitted, **Then** two distinct `Missing Coupons` discrepancies are created — one per booklet, each listing that booklet's own missing coupons — and both appear in the entry's `discrepancies_detected` table.

### User Story 2 - Auto-Detect Coupons From an Unassigned Booklet (Priority: P1)

A delivery man hands in a coupon whose parent booklet has never been sold — the booklet is still `In Stock`, `In Custody`, or (theoretically) `Depleted`. Either the shop made a record-keeping error (someone forgot to run the sale before handing out the booklet) or a delivery man is pulling coupons from stock that was never assigned to any customer. Both possibilities matter, and the system flags them as a distinct discrepancy type. On Consumption Entry submit, after the coupon is marked `Consumed` in the normal Phase 4 way, the system creates one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` per offending coupon with `discrepancy_type = "Unassigned Booklet"`, status `Open`, linked to the triggering entry and the booklet. Because the booklet has no customer, the discrepancy's `customer` field stays null. The `affected_coupons` list contains just the coupon that was submitted (no gap analysis — this is a different anomaly). The Administrator sees the same non-blocking modal as in Story 1.

**Why this priority**: Unassigned-booklet detection is the twin of missing-coupons detection and is similarly mandatory to ship the phase. A shop that sells booklets but cannot detect coupons coming in from booklets that were never officially sold has a loophole wide enough to drive every leakage through. Same P1 tier as Story 1 because the two detections together are the minimal "intelligence" the phase exists to add; shipping one without the other leaves the system half-blind.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by seeding one delivery man and one booklet `WP-00002` that is **not** `Sold` (still `In Stock`, with all 20 coupons `Available`). Submit a Consumption Entry for `CP-00025` (a coupon from `WP-00002`). Verify the coupon transitions to `Consumed` (Phase 4 side effects still fire), and verify exactly one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` is created with `discrepancy_type = "Unassigned Booklet"`, `status = "Open"`, `booklet = WP-00002`, `customer` null, `affected_coupons` containing only `CP-00025`, `triggering_consumption_entry` pointing at the entry, and the entry's `has_warnings = True` with `discrepancies_detected` linking to the new discrepancy.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** booklet `WP-00002` is `In Stock` with all coupons `Available`, **When** the Administrator submits a Consumption Entry containing `CP-00025` (a coupon of `WP-00002`), **Then** the entry succeeds, `CP-00025` becomes `Consumed`, and exactly one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` of type `Unassigned Booklet` is created for `CP-00025` with `customer = null`, `status = Open`, and `affected_coupons` containing only that one coupon.
2. **Given** booklet `WP-00003` is `In Custody` of a delivery man (not yet sold), **When** any of its coupons is submitted in a Consumption Entry, **Then** an `Unassigned Booklet` discrepancy is created — the rule is "booklet status is not `Sold`", not specifically "booklet is `In Stock`".
3. **Given** a single Consumption Entry contains three coupons `CP-00025`, `CP-00026`, `CP-00027` all from the same unassigned booklet `WP-00002`, **When** the entry is submitted, **Then** three distinct `Unassigned Booklet` discrepancies are created — one per coupon — because each submitted coupon from an unassigned booklet is an independent anomaly (the PRD models this as per-coupon, not per-booklet, to allow per-coupon financial resolution).
4. **Given** a single Consumption Entry contains `CP-00001` (from a `Sold` booklet `WP-00001`, part of a legitimate in-sequence consumption) and `CP-00025` (from `In Stock` booklet `WP-00002`), **When** the entry is submitted, **Then** `CP-00001` is consumed silently with no discrepancy, and exactly one `Unassigned Booklet` discrepancy is created for `CP-00025`.
5. **Given** the same Consumption Entry triggers both a `Missing Coupons` discrepancy (Story 1) and an `Unassigned Booklet` discrepancy (Story 2) from different booklets, **When** the entry is submitted, **Then** both discrepancies are created, both are listed in the entry's `discrepancies_detected` table, and both appear in the post-submit modal dialog.

### User Story 3 - Resolve a Discrepancy as Administrator Error (Priority: P2)

Not every flagged anomaly is someone's fault. Sometimes a booklet was handed out without anyone first running the sale; sometimes coupons were given to a customer out of order legitimately; sometimes the shop itself made the bookkeeping mistake. For these cases the Administrator opens the `Open` discrepancy record, reviews it, sets `resolution_action = "None"`, optionally writes a note in `resolution_notes` explaining what happened, and submits. The discrepancy is now permanently recorded (and locked — see Story 6), but no Journal Entry and no Payment Entry is created; the books are unaffected. The discrepancy's `status` becomes `Resolved - Admin Error` and `resolved_on` is stamped with the submit time.

**Why this priority**: Admin-error resolution is the lightest of the three resolution paths but is the one most often used in practice (most discrepancies turn out to be bookkeeping slips, not theft). P2 rather than P1 because detection (Stories 1 + 2) must exist before anything needs resolving, but it is still required for the phase to be usable — without a way to close out discrepancies, the "Open Discrepancies" list grows forever. Within the resolution tier this is the path with zero financial integration, so it is the least risky and the most straightforward to ship first.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by seeding one `Open` discrepancy (via the detection path of Story 1 or 2). Open the discrepancy record, set `resolution_action = "None"`, optionally set a resolution note, and submit. Verify `status` transitions to `Resolved - Admin Error`, `resolved_on` is populated with a timestamp near the current moment, no `Journal Entry` was created, no `Payment Entry` was created, the `journal_entry` and `payment_entry` link fields on the discrepancy remain empty, and the record is now locked (see Story 6).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy `PCD-2026-00001` exists, **When** the Administrator submits it with `resolution_action = "None"`, **Then** its `status` becomes `Resolved - Admin Error`, `resolved_on` is set to the submit datetime, and no financial document is created anywhere in ERPNext.
2. **Given** an `Open` Unassigned Booklet discrepancy exists, **When** the Administrator submits it with `resolution_action = "None"` but no `liable_delivery_man`, **Then** the submit succeeds — no liable delivery man is required for the Admin Error path.
3. **Given** an `Open` discrepancy with a non-empty `resolution_notes`, **When** the Administrator submits it with `resolution_action = "None"`, **Then** the notes are preserved on the submitted record and remain visible in the locked state.

### User Story 4 - Resolve a Discrepancy by Adding to Delivery Man's Liability Ledger (Priority: P2)

When the Administrator concludes that a specific delivery man is responsible for the missing or misappropriated coupons, the delivery man owes the shop the coupon value. The Administrator opens the discrepancy, picks `liable_delivery_man` from the `related_delivery_men` list (or from any Employee), optionally edits `estimated_amount` (defaults to auto-computed `coupon_unit_price × affected_coupons_count`), sets `resolution_action = "Add to Liability Ledger"`, and submits. The system automatically creates a standard ERPNext Journal Entry: a debit against the configured employee-liability account (with `party_type = Employee`, `party = liable_delivery_man`), and a credit against the configured discrepancy-offset account, for the discrepancy's amount. The created Journal Entry is linked back to the discrepancy via its `journal_entry` field. The discrepancy status becomes `Resolved - Delivery Man Liable` and `resolved_on` is stamped. The amount now shows up on the delivery man's employee-ledger balance and can later be deducted through a standard ERPNext Salary Slip adjustment — no extra Purisol code is needed for the salary integration, that is ERPNext's job.

**Why this priority**: This is the path with teeth — the one that actually moves money on the books and holds delivery men accountable. It is what the shop owner will use whenever a delivery man cannot reasonably claim the discrepancy was an admin error. P2 because it still sits on top of detection and it is not usable without the settings accounts configured, but it is the most business-critical of the resolution paths — the shop's whole reason for wanting discrepancy tracking is to recoup losses, and this is the mechanism.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by (a) pre-configuring `Purisol Settings.employee_liability_account` and `.discrepancy_offset_account` with two real GL accounts; (b) seeding one `Open` discrepancy with `affected_coupons` count = 4 and an auto-computed `estimated_amount`; (c) opening the discrepancy, setting `liable_delivery_man` to a seeded Employee, setting `resolution_action = "Add to Liability Ledger"`, and submitting. Verify a new `Journal Entry` is created and submitted with the correct debit account + party / credit account / amount; verify the JE is linked via the discrepancy's `journal_entry` field; verify the discrepancy's `status = "Resolved - Delivery Man Liable"` and `resolved_on` is set; verify the employee's liability account GL balance increases by the discrepancy amount.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an `Open` discrepancy with `estimated_amount = 40.00`, and `Purisol Settings` has `employee_liability_account = "Employee Liability - Co"` and `discrepancy_offset_account = "Sales Adjustment - Co"`, **When** the Administrator sets `liable_delivery_man = Ali` and `resolution_action = "Add to Liability Ledger"` and submits, **Then** a Journal Entry is created with one debit line (account = `Employee Liability - Co`, party_type = `Employee`, party = `Ali`, amount = 40.00) and one credit line (account = `Sales Adjustment - Co`, amount = 40.00); the JE's name is stored in the discrepancy's `journal_entry` field; the discrepancy's `status = "Resolved - Delivery Man Liable"` and `resolved_on` is stamped.
2. **Given** an `Open` discrepancy, **When** the Administrator submits with `resolution_action = "Add to Liability Ledger"` but without `liable_delivery_man` set, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear error identifying the missing field; no JE is created; the discrepancy remains `Open`.
3. **Given** an `Open` discrepancy with auto-computed `estimated_amount = 40.00`, **When** the Administrator manually edits the amount to `35.00` before submitting with `Add to Liability Ledger`, **Then** the created Journal Entry uses the edited amount (`35.00`), not the original auto-computed value.
4. **Given** `Purisol Settings.employee_liability_account` is not configured, **When** the Administrator submits a discrepancy with `Add to Liability Ledger`, **Then** the submit fails with a clear error pointing at the missing setting; no partial JE is created.

### User Story 5 - Resolve a Discrepancy via Immediate Cash Payment (Priority: P2)

When the delivery man settles the shortage on the spot (paid in cash at the shop), the Administrator still records the discrepancy but closes it through a Payment Entry instead of a Journal Entry. The Administrator opens the discrepancy, sets `liable_delivery_man`, optionally edits `estimated_amount`, sets `resolution_action = "Immediate Cash Payment"`, and submits. The system creates a standard ERPNext Payment Entry (payment_type "Receive") with cash coming in to the configured default cash account and flowing against the discrepancy-offset account, for the discrepancy's amount. The Payment Entry references the discrepancy record so the receipt is traceable. The discrepancy's `status` becomes `Resolved - Paid`, `resolved_on` is stamped, and the `payment_entry` link field is populated.

**Why this priority**: Cash-payment resolution is the third path and is operationally common in small-shop environments where delivery men are confronted with shortages and settle immediately. Same P2 tier as Story 4 because it is one of the two financial resolutions; it is equally essential to MVP and uses the same `liable_delivery_man` requirement but a different ERPNext primitive (Payment Entry, not Journal Entry).

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by (a) pre-configuring `Purisol Settings.discrepancy_offset_account` and `.default_cash_account`; (b) seeding one `Open` discrepancy; (c) opening the discrepancy, setting `liable_delivery_man` to a seeded Employee, setting `resolution_action = "Immediate Cash Payment"`, and submitting. Verify a new `Payment Entry` is created with payment_type "Receive", `paid_from = discrepancy_offset_account`, `paid_to = default_cash_account`, `paid_amount = estimated_amount`, and a reference pointing at the discrepancy; verify the PE is linked via the discrepancy's `payment_entry` field; verify the discrepancy's `status = "Resolved - Paid"` and `resolved_on` is set.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an `Open` discrepancy with `estimated_amount = 40.00`, and `Purisol Settings` has `discrepancy_offset_account = "Sales Adjustment - Co"` and `default_cash_account = "Cash - Co"`, **When** the Administrator sets `liable_delivery_man = Ali` and `resolution_action = "Immediate Cash Payment"` and submits, **Then** a Payment Entry is created with `payment_type = "Receive"`, `paid_from = "Sales Adjustment - Co"`, `paid_to = "Cash - Co"`, `paid_amount = 40.00`, and references the discrepancy; the discrepancy's `payment_entry` field holds the PE name; `status = "Resolved - Paid"`, `resolved_on` is stamped.
2. **Given** an `Open` discrepancy, **When** the Administrator submits with `resolution_action = "Immediate Cash Payment"` but without `liable_delivery_man`, **Then** the submit is rejected with a clear error; no Payment Entry is created.
3. **Given** `Purisol Settings.default_cash_account` is not configured, **When** the Administrator submits a discrepancy with `Immediate Cash Payment`, **Then** the submit fails with a clear error; no partial PE is created.
4. **Given** a discrepancy is resolved via cash payment, **When** any standard ERPNext cash-flow or payment report is run, **Then** the Payment Entry appears in those reports exactly as any other payment would.

### User Story 6 - Surface Discrepancies for Investigation (Priority: P3)

Detection and resolution are the core mechanics, but by themselves they are silent — a discrepancy could be created and never noticed. Phase 5 therefore gives the Administrator three investigation aids, all of which sit on top of Stories 1–5 and make them usable. First, **the post-submit modal dialog**: when a Consumption Entry is submitted and discrepancies are created, a non-blocking dialog pops up on the Administrator's screen summarising what was opened (type, booklet, affected coupons, link to each new discrepancy record). Second, **the "Open Discrepancies" list view**: a saved standard ERPNext list filtered to `status = Open`, available under the Purisol menu, so the Administrator can see at a glance everything awaiting resolution. Third, **the `related_delivery_men` auto-population**: when a discrepancy is created, the system pre-fills a child table listing every delivery man who had a touchpoint with the affected booklet — the current custody holder (if still in custody) tagged `Custody Holder`, plus every delivery man who submitted consumption entries for the same booklet in the last 30 days tagged `Submitted Adjacent Coupons`, with back-references to the specific custody entry or consumption entry that caused them to show up. The Administrator uses this table as a shortlist when deciding whom to hold liable. Once a discrepancy is submitted, it locks permanently — per standard ERPNext submittable-doc semantics — so Stories 3, 4, and 5 produce records that cannot later be edited (corrections require reversing JEs or PEs).

**Why this priority**: These are the "UX wrappers" around the core detection-and-resolution engine. Detection and resolution are testable on their own via script and list queries, so these surfaces are strictly P3 — nice to have, not blocking. But the Administrator experience is noticeably degraded without them: without the modal, they won't know a discrepancy was just opened until they manually check the list; without auto-populated `related_delivery_men`, every resolution involves manual Employee lookups; without the locked-after-submit contract, the audit trail the constitution demands is not guaranteed. P3 captures "this is a real part of the feature, but it can be built and tested after the engine below it works".

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by (a) seeding one booklet `WP-00001` with prior custody by delivery man Ali (still `In Custody`) and prior consumption entries from delivery man Bilal submitted 10 days ago; (b) submitting a Consumption Entry that triggers a Missing Coupons discrepancy for `WP-00001`; (c) verifying the new discrepancy's `related_delivery_men` table has two rows — Ali with role `Custody Holder` (reference to the custody entry), Bilal with role `Submitted Adjacent Coupons` (reference to his earlier consumption entry); (d) verifying a post-submit modal (or structured API response the UI renders as a modal) lists the newly-opened discrepancy; (e) opening the "Open Discrepancies" list view and verifying the new discrepancy appears; (f) submitting the discrepancy via Story 3's path, then attempting to edit any field on the now-locked record and confirming the system rejects the edit.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` is currently `In Custody` of Ali, and delivery man Bilal submitted a Consumption Entry 10 days ago for coupons from `WP-00001`, **When** a new Consumption Entry triggers a Missing Coupons discrepancy for `WP-00001`, **Then** the discrepancy's `related_delivery_men` table contains exactly two rows: (Ali, `Custody Holder`, reference = Ali's custody entry) and (Bilal, `Submitted Adjacent Coupons`, reference = Bilal's consumption entry).
2. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` is `Sold` (not in custody) and no other delivery man has submitted consumption entries for it, **When** a Missing Coupons discrepancy is created, **Then** `related_delivery_men` contains only the triggering entry's delivery man (tagged `Submitted Adjacent Coupons`) — `Custody Holder` is omitted when the booklet is not `In Custody`.
3. **Given** booklet `WP-00001` had consumption entries from three different delivery men over the last 45 days, **When** a new discrepancy is created, **Then** `related_delivery_men` lists only the delivery men whose entries fell within the last 30 days from the triggering entry's posting date.
4. **Given** a Consumption Entry just submitted and created two new discrepancies, **When** the submit completes, **Then** a modal dialog (or equivalent UI surface) is shown to the Administrator listing both discrepancies by name, type, booklet, and affected coupon count, each with a click-through to the discrepancy record.
5. **Given** the Administrator opens the "Open Discrepancies" list view, **When** the list loads, **Then** it shows every `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` with `status = Open`, ordered by `opened_on` descending, with columns for discrepancy_type, booklet, customer, estimated_amount, and opened_on.
6. **Given** a discrepancy has been submitted with `resolution_action = "None"`, **When** the Administrator attempts to edit any field on it, **Then** the system prevents the edit with the standard ERPNext "cannot edit submitted document" message; amending is also blocked.

### Edge Cases

- **Gap below the consumed minimum**: A booklet has coupons `CP-00006`, `CP-00007` consumed and `CP-00001`–`CP-00005` still `Available`. The union of consumed coupons is `{6, 7}`, min = 6, max = 7, **no interior gap**. No Missing Coupons discrepancy fires, even though five coupons are "skipped" at the low end. This is intentional — the PRD defines the anomaly as a gap **between** consumed coupons, not as any unconsumed prefix. A shop that consumes coupons out of order from the start will surface the discrepancy only once at least one lower coupon is eventually consumed.
- **Gap above the consumed maximum**: Same logic applies at the top end. If coupons `CP-00015`–`CP-00020` are still `Available` after `CP-00014` is consumed, no gap is flagged; those coupons are simply "not yet consumed", which is legitimate until the booklet depletes.
- **Single-coupon submission on an unassigned booklet with later gaps**: If `WP-00002` is `In Stock` and the Administrator submits only `CP-00025`, an `Unassigned Booklet` discrepancy fires for that coupon. If they later (after the booklet is retroactively sold) submit `CP-00030` with `CP-00026`–`CP-00029` still `Available`, a `Missing Coupons` discrepancy fires for those four — the two detection rules are independent and can both fire against the same booklet across different entries.
- **Exact-same gap re-detected**: If a later consumption entry does not extend the min-or-max of a booklet's consumed set, the gap computed is identical to a prior one. Dedup logic excludes coupons already covered by an existing `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy for the same booklet, so no duplicate discrepancy is created. If the dedup filter empties the gap set entirely, no discrepancy is created at all.
- **Partial overlap between previously-detected gap and newly-revealed gap**: Booklet has an `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy for `CP-00009`, `CP-00010`. A new entry consumes `CP-00012`. The fresh gap is `{9, 10, 11}`; after dedup, only `CP-00011` is new. A new Missing Coupons discrepancy is created listing only `CP-00011`. The existing `Open` discrepancy is untouched.
- **Discrepancy on an already-Depleted booklet**: If a booklet reaches `Depleted` (all 20 coupons `Consumed`) while an `Open` discrepancy for it is still unresolved, the discrepancy remains `Open`. The booklet's terminal state does not auto-resolve related discrepancies — the shop still needs to decide who was liable.
- **Amendment attempt on a locked discrepancy**: ERPNext submittable docs can normally be amended (cancel + new version); Phase 5 disallows amending a resolved discrepancy — corrective action goes through a separate reversing JE or PE, not through re-opening the discrepancy. This matches PRD section 8.6.
- **Settings accounts change between detection and resolution**: If the Administrator changes `employee_liability_account` on `Purisol Settings` after a discrepancy is opened but before it is resolved, the resolution uses the current settings value at submit time (not the value that existed when the discrepancy was opened). Discrepancies do not snapshot account settings.
- **Consumption entry with zero discrepancies detected**: The flow is strictly Phase 4 — `has_warnings` stays `False`, `discrepancies_detected` stays empty, no modal fires, all side effects complete as before. Phase 5 only adds behaviour when at least one anomaly is found.
- **Cancellation of a consumption entry that created discrepancies**: Out of scope for MVP. Standard ERPNext cancellation on the consumption entry does not automatically cancel or modify the linked discrepancy records; the Administrator handles those through the discrepancy resolution flow. (This parallels the Phase 3 decision that booklet-sale cancellation is blocked once coupons are consumed.)

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST scan every Coupon Consumption Entry for discrepancies as part of the submit transaction — detection happens after Phase 4's blocking validations and coupon/booklet updates, within the same database transaction.
- **FR-002**: Discrepancy detection MUST NOT block the consumption entry submission — all detected anomalies are non-blocking warnings; the entry still commits every Phase 4 side effect (coupon status, booklet counts, auto-depletion).
- **FR-003**: System MUST detect **Missing Coupons** by, for each distinct booklet touched by the entry, computing the union of (previously-consumed coupons) ∪ (coupons being consumed in this entry), identifying any coupon number strictly between that set's min and max whose current status is `Available`, and creating one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` per affected booklet with `discrepancy_type = "Missing Coupons"` listing those missing coupons in `affected_coupons`.
- **FR-004**: System MUST detect **Unassigned Booklet** by, for each coupon in the entry whose parent booklet has `status != "Sold"`, creating one `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` per such coupon with `discrepancy_type = "Unassigned Booklet"` and `affected_coupons` containing only that coupon.
- **FR-005**: For each created discrepancy, system MUST auto-populate `status = "Open"`, `opened_on = now()`, `triggering_consumption_entry = <the submitting entry>`, `booklet = <the affected booklet>`, and `customer` equal to the booklet's `customer` (null when the booklet is not `Sold`, as in Unassigned Booklet cases).
- **FR-006**: System MUST auto-compute `estimated_amount` on each created discrepancy as `coupon_unit_price × count(affected_coupons)`, where `coupon_unit_price` is derived per the Assumptions section. The field remains editable by the Administrator until the discrepancy is submitted.
- **FR-007**: System MUST auto-populate `related_delivery_men` on each created discrepancy with (a) one row tagged `Custody Holder` referencing the booklet's current custody holder and their triggering custody entry, but only if the booklet is currently `In Custody`; and (b) one row per distinct delivery man who submitted any Coupon Consumption Entry referencing the same booklet in the last 30 days from the triggering entry's posting date, each tagged `Submitted Adjacent Coupons` and referencing that specific consumption entry.
- **FR-008**: When one or more discrepancies are created during a Consumption Entry submit, system MUST set that entry's `has_warnings = True` and populate its `discrepancies_detected` child table with links to each created discrepancy — all within the same transaction as the consumption entry's other side effects.
- **FR-009**: On Consumption Entry submit with discrepancies created, system MUST surface a non-blocking UI dialog to the Administrator summarising the newly-opened discrepancies, each with a click-through link to the discrepancy record; dismissing the dialog does not affect the submitted state.
- **FR-010**: Missing Coupons detection MUST be idempotent across submits: a coupon already listed in an `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy for the same booklet MUST be excluded from any new Missing Coupons discrepancy; if all would-be-listed coupons are already covered, no new discrepancy is created.
- **FR-011**: System MUST enforce that a `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` submit with `resolution_action = "None"` succeeds without requiring `liable_delivery_man`, sets `status = "Resolved - Admin Error"`, sets `resolved_on = now()`, and creates no financial document.
- **FR-012**: System MUST enforce that a `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` submit with `resolution_action = "Add to Liability Ledger"` requires `liable_delivery_man` (submit fails with a clear error if unset) and requires `Purisol Settings.employee_liability_account` and `Purisol Settings.discrepancy_offset_account` to both be configured (submit fails with a clear error if either is missing).
- **FR-013**: On `Add to Liability Ledger` submit, system MUST create an ERPNext `Journal Entry` with one debit line (account = `Purisol Settings.employee_liability_account`, `party_type = "Employee"`, `party = liable_delivery_man`, amount = `estimated_amount`) and one credit line (account = `Purisol Settings.discrepancy_offset_account`, amount = `estimated_amount`), store the created JE's name in the discrepancy's `journal_entry` field, set discrepancy `status = "Resolved - Delivery Man Liable"`, and stamp `resolved_on = now()` — all within a single transaction. If any step fails, the whole resolution submit MUST roll back.
- **FR-014**: System MUST enforce that a `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` submit with `resolution_action = "Immediate Cash Payment"` requires `liable_delivery_man` and requires `Purisol Settings.discrepancy_offset_account` and `Purisol Settings.default_cash_account` to both be configured (submit fails with a clear error otherwise).
- **FR-015**: On `Immediate Cash Payment` submit, system MUST create an ERPNext `Payment Entry` with `payment_type = "Receive"`, `paid_from = Purisol Settings.discrepancy_offset_account`, `paid_to = Purisol Settings.default_cash_account`, `paid_amount = estimated_amount`, and a reference row pointing at the discrepancy; store the created PE's name in the discrepancy's `payment_entry` field; set discrepancy `status = "Resolved - Paid"`; and stamp `resolved_on = now()` — all within a single transaction.
- **FR-016**: System MUST persist the Administrator's `resolution_notes` verbatim on submit regardless of which `resolution_action` is chosen.
- **FR-017**: Submitted (resolved) `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` records MUST be immutable — all fields are locked under standard ERPNext submittable-document semantics; amendment is not permitted and any correction goes through a separate reversing Journal Entry or Payment Entry.
- **FR-018**: System MUST provide an "Open Discrepancies" list view available to the `Purisol Administrator` role, filtered to `status = "Open"` and sorted by `opened_on` descending, with columns for discrepancy_type, booklet, customer, estimated_amount, and opened_on.
- **FR-019**: Every created discrepancy, its detection context, and its eventual resolution (resolution_action, resolved_on, linked JE/PE, resolution_notes) MUST be fully recoverable from the `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` record itself, satisfying the constitution's audit-trail requirement — no state change happens as a silent field update.
- **FR-020**: A single Consumption Entry submit that detects both Missing Coupons and Unassigned Booklet anomalies MUST create all applicable discrepancies (one Missing Coupons per affected booklet; one Unassigned Booklet per offending coupon), all linked in the entry's `discrepancies_detected` table, and surfaced together in the post-submit dialog.
- **FR-021**: Discrepancy detection MUST only introspect the submitting Consumption Entry; it MUST NOT mutate any booklet, coupon, or consumption entry record (all mutations to those entities belong to Phase 4's logic).

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Purisol Coupon Discrepancy**: A submittable investigation record opened automatically by the system and resolved manually by the Administrator. Carries `discrepancy_type` (`Missing Coupons` | `Unassigned Booklet`), `status` (`Open` | `Under Review` | `Resolved - Admin Error` | `Resolved - Delivery Man Liable` | `Resolved - Paid`), timestamps (`opened_on`, `resolved_on`), references to the `triggering_consumption_entry`, `booklet`, and (optionally) `customer`, a list of `affected_coupons`, a list of `related_delivery_men`, a `liable_delivery_man` set at resolution time, an `estimated_amount` (auto-computed, editable pre-submit), the chosen `resolution_action`, the resulting `journal_entry` or `payment_entry` link, and `resolution_notes`. Submitted records are locked.
- **Discrepancy Coupon (child of Purisol Coupon Discrepancy)**: One row per affected coupon. For Missing Coupons, each row refers to an `Available` coupon in the detected gap. For Unassigned Booklet, a single row refers to the coupon that was submitted. Rows hold the coupon number and optional notes.
- **Discrepancy Related Person (child of Purisol Coupon Discrepancy)**: One row per delivery man with a known touchpoint to the affected booklet. Each row has the delivery man (Employee), a role (`Custody Holder` or `Submitted Adjacent Coupons`), and a reference to the triggering custody or consumption entry. Used as an investigation shortlist when selecting `liable_delivery_man`.
- **Consumption Entry's Discrepancies-Detected Child Table (reuse from Phase 4 reserved stub)**: One row per discrepancy created during this entry's submit. Exists so that navigating from the consumption entry immediately surfaces every anomaly it caused; paired with `has_warnings` flag on the parent entry.
- **Linked ERPNext Journal Entry**: Created as a side effect of `Add to Liability Ledger` resolution; standard ERPNext primitive. Debit line targets the employee-liability account with the liable delivery man as the party; credit line targets the discrepancy-offset account.
- **Linked ERPNext Payment Entry**: Created as a side effect of `Immediate Cash Payment` resolution; standard ERPNext primitive. Payment type "Receive"; flows cash from the discrepancy-offset account into the default cash account; references the discrepancy.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Submitting coupons `CP-00007`, `CP-00008` from a `Sold` booklet with `CP-00003`–`CP-00006` still `Available` produces exactly one `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy listing those four coupons, with `estimated_amount = 4 × coupon_unit_price`, within a single transaction.
- **SC-002**: Submitting a coupon from a booklet whose status is not `Sold` produces exactly one `Open` Unassigned Booklet discrepancy for that coupon, with `customer = null`, within a single transaction.
- **SC-003**: A single Consumption Entry that detects both anomaly types simultaneously creates all applicable discrepancies in one transaction — none are missed, none are duplicated — and the entry's `discrepancies_detected` table references every created discrepancy.
- **SC-004**: A re-submit that would re-detect a Missing Coupons gap already fully covered by an `Open` discrepancy creates zero new discrepancy records (idempotence holds 100% across repeated submits for the same gap).
- **SC-005**: Resolving a discrepancy with `Add to Liability Ledger` produces a Journal Entry whose debit + credit accounts, party, and amount exactly match the configured settings and the discrepancy's `estimated_amount`, and the JE appears in standard ERPNext GL and employee-ledger reports.
- **SC-006**: Resolving a discrepancy with `Immediate Cash Payment` produces a Payment Entry whose paid_from, paid_to, amount, and reference exactly match the configured settings and the discrepancy, and the PE appears in standard ERPNext cash-flow reports.
- **SC-007**: Resolving a discrepancy with `resolution_action = "None"` creates zero financial documents and leaves every GL account balance unchanged.
- **SC-008**: After a discrepancy is submitted (via any of the three resolution paths), 100% of attempts to edit any of its fields are rejected by the system.
- **SC-009**: An Administrator opening the "Open Discrepancies" list view sees every current `Open` discrepancy and zero resolved ones; the list refreshes after each new resolution without requiring a reload beyond standard ERPNext list behaviour.
- **SC-010**: A Consumption Entry that creates at least one discrepancy surfaces the post-submit modal dialog listing every discrepancy created in that submit, with a click-through link per entry, in 100% of submits that produce discrepancies.
- **SC-011**: For any discrepancy, `related_delivery_men` contains exactly the booklet's current custody holder (if `In Custody`) plus every distinct delivery man who submitted a Consumption Entry referencing the same booklet within 30 days of the triggering entry's posting date — no more, no fewer.
- **SC-012**: A consumption entry that triggers zero discrepancies runs in the same wall-clock time envelope as Phase 4 (detection adds no noticeable latency for discrepancy-free entries) and behaves identically to Phase 4 from the Administrator's perspective.

## Assumptions

- **`coupon_unit_price` source**: Phase 5 derives the per-coupon unit price used in `estimated_amount` as follows: (1) if the affected booklet's `sales_invoice` is set, take the rate from the matching Sales Invoice Item line (the line whose `purisol_booklet` custom field points at this booklet) and divide by 20 (one booklet = 20 coupons); (2) otherwise, fall back to the current default-price-list rate for `Purisol Settings.coupon_item` divided by 20; (3) if neither is resolvable (e.g., Unassigned Booklet with no invoice and an unconfigured price list entry), `estimated_amount` defaults to 0 and the Administrator edits it manually before resolving. This assumption keeps Phase 5 self-contained and reuses Phase 3's Sales Invoice integration.
- **Missing-coupons dedup policy**: A coupon that already appears in the `affected_coupons` of any `Open` Missing Coupons discrepancy for the same booklet is excluded from any newly-computed gap set. This prevents duplicate discrepancies for the same underlying gap and bounds the "Open Discrepancies" list to one row per distinct missing coupon per booklet. Resolved discrepancies do not provide this protection — once a Missing Coupons discrepancy is resolved, the same missing coupon, if it remains `Available`, could legitimately trigger a new discrepancy on a future entry (rare; happens only if the admin resolves but the physical gap persists).
- **`related_delivery_men` lookback window**: The 30-day window anchors to the triggering consumption entry's `posting_date`, not to `now()`. This keeps detection deterministic even if the entry is posted with a back-dated date.
- **`Under Review` status is administrator-optional**: The `Under Review` status is settable via Save (not Submit) on an `Open` discrepancy as an investigation marker; it is not a required step on the path from `Open` to any `Resolved - *` state. No business logic hinges on it.
- **Locked-after-submit**: Locking follows standard ERPNext submittable-doc semantics — no custom lock logic in Phase 5. Amendment (standard ERPNext cancel + new version workflow) is explicitly disabled for `Purisol Coupon Discrepancy` to match the constitution's "audit trail by default" principle; corrections are booked through separate reversing Journal Entries / Payment Entries against the same accounts.
- **Out of scope: notifications.** The post-submit modal on the Consumption Entry form is a UI-level surface only. Bell-icon notifications, emails, and other channels are Phase 6 — the `Purisol Notify` abstraction referenced in Phase 6 does not exist yet in Phase 5.
- **Out of scope: reports.** Discrepancy-related reports (delivery-man discrepancy counts, rates, liability balance) are Phase 7. Phase 5 ships only a basic standard-ERPNext "Open Discrepancies" list view.
- **Out of scope: retroactive customer assignment.** PRD section 8.6 mentions an optional "Retroactive Customer Assignment" action when an Unassigned Booklet discrepancy is resolved as Admin Error. That separate action (creating a backdated Sales Invoice to retroactively attach a customer to the booklet) is **not** part of Phase 5 — it is a later feature; in Phase 5, resolving with `None` simply closes the discrepancy and the booklet's status remains whatever it was. This keeps Phase 5 scope bounded.
- **Out of scope: amending or cancelling a consumption entry after discrepancies exist.** If an Administrator cancels a consumption entry that created discrepancies, the discrepancies are not auto-cancelled or updated; they are handled manually through the resolution flow. This matches the "submitted records are source of truth" constitution principle.
- **Dependency: Phase 4 is fully shipped.** Phase 5 hooks into Consumption Entry submit after Phase 4's blocking validations and core side effects have committed. If Phase 4 tests fail, Phase 5 tests will fail transitively — that is acceptable and expected.
- **Dependency: Purisol Settings account fields.** The three account fields (`employee_liability_account`, `discrepancy_offset_account`, `default_cash_account`) exist on `Purisol Settings` from Phase 1. Phase 5 adds no new configuration fields; it only starts using the ones already reserved.
- **Dependency: `Purisol Discrepancy Coupon` and `Purisol Discrepancy Related Person` child-table shapes per PRD 7.5.** Phase 5 creates these DocTypes (or fills in the stub reserved in Phase 4's implementation) with the PRD-specified fields; no additional custom fields are introduced in this phase.
